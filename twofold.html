Before you book

Questions, answered

Do I need to bring outfits?

No. The Twofold comes with its outfits: the fur coat for the Coat Code and the wings and white look for Angelic Muse. After booking, please send us your dress size so we can check which outfits are available for you. We have studio shoes in size 6 (beige) and sizes 7 and 9 (black), or you can bring your own.

I've never had a photoshoot. Is that okay?

Yes. Most of our clients are first-timers. Your photographer directs every pose and checks the shots with you as you go.

When should I arrive?

Please arrive 1.5 hours before your schedule for hair and makeup. Arriving later may shorten your actual shoot.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Rescheduling at least 5 days before your shoot is free. Rescheduling 4 days or less before your shoot, including on the day itself, has a ₱200 rescheduling fee. Your new date must be in the same month as your original booking for your deposit to stay valid.

What if I cancel or miss my session?

If you cancel at least 5 days before your shoot, your ₱300 deposit can be used for another package scheduled within the same month. Cancelling less than 5 days before your shoot forfeits the deposit. If you miss your session, your deposit can still be used for a shoot within the same month.

Can I get my photos faster?

Rush delivery is available as an add-on. Ask the studio when you book.
