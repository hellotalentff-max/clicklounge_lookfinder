Before you book

Good to know

How do I reserve my slot?

Pick a date on the booking page, then send a ₱300 deposit through GCash or BPI and enter the reference number. The studio confirms your booking by email.

What should I wear?

Bring your own outfit, or rent a gown from our House of Roan collection. A gown is included with Prestige; for the other sessions it is an optional rental. The quiz shows gowns that fit your sizes.

How long does the shoot take?

Each session card shows the shoot time. Plan to arrive a little early, and allow extra time if you are changing outfits.

What if I need to cancel or move my booking?

Message us as early as you can. If you cancel or miss your session, your ₱300 deposit can still be used for another shoot booked within the same month as your original date.

Can I get my photos faster?

Rush delivery is available as an add-on. Ask the studio when you book.
